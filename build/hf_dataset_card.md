The data behind the [Open Source AI Gap Map](https://map.currentai.org/): $n_products products
from $n_organizations organizations across $n_categories categories of the AI stack, each graded
on openness, adoption and capability, with every score traced to a cited primary source.

Join keys: `products.slug` = `citations.product_slug` = `product_history.current_slug`;
`products.organization_slug` = `organizations.slug`; `products.category_slug` = `categories.slug`.
In the history tables `product_slug` is the slug as that release published it; join on
`current_slug` to follow a renamed product to its row in `products`.

### `products`

Each product sits in exactly one category and one organization. Columns are grouped by axis:

- **Openness** (`openness_*`): a 0–5 grade against openness frameworks (the Model Openness
  Framework for models, OSI license classes for software). `openness_class` is the named class
  (`open_source`, `open_weights`, `restricted`, ...) and `openness_bucket` collapses it to
  `open`, `open-ish` or `closed`. `openness_components` lists the license, access and
  documentation findings behind the grade.
- **Adoption** (`adoption_*`): a 0–5 level from real usage (downloads, active users,
  deployments), not stars. `adoption_reach` is the band the level rests on, such as `1M-10M`.
  Raw measurements are not published here.
- **Capability** (`capability_*`): a 0–5 grade from community benchmarks where they exist and
  feature coverage where they don't. `capability_relative_to` and `capability_relation` name the
  peer product the grade was set against.
- **Overall** (`overall_score`, `overall_basis`, `tier`, `mature`): adoption and capability
  blended with the category's weights. `tier` is `leading` (≥ 4.5), `strong` (4.0–4.5) or null.
  `overall_basis` says whether a score blends both axes or rests on adoption alone; don't compare
  the two kinds directly.

Every axis carries a `*_confidence`, a `*_note` explaining the grade in prose, and a
`*_last_verified` date. Artifact columns (`github`, `pypi`, `huggingface_model`, ...) list the
URLs the product is identified and measured by. `lineage_*` columns name the products (by slug)
or external works a product derives from, trains, or belongs to.

### `categories`

`stage_num` / `stage_name` is the category's Maturity Stage, from 0 (Void) to 5 (Mature), and
`gaps` names what the open ecosystem still lacks there (`adoption`, `capability`, `openness`,
...). Both are derived from the category's products; the rules are in the methodology.

### `citations`

The audit trail. Each row is one source cited for one axis of one product: the `url`, what the
page `shows` (a short quotation or paraphrase), when it was `accessed`, the `http_status` seen,
and a `content_sha256` of the fetched bytes so a reader can tell whether the page has changed
since. `establishes` names the facts the source was cited for, where recorded.

### `product_history` and `category_history`

What each tagged release published, read from that release's build output in git, from
v$first_release onward ($n_releases releases in this export). Rows carry the release `version`,
its `released` date, the `payload_generated` date of the build output, and the `source_commit`.

**Read changes between releases with care.** A value can move because the product changed, because
new evidence arrived, or because the method changed: categories were added and split, adoption
bands were re-drawn, and the taxonomy was revised. The repository's
[CHANGELOG](https://github.com/currentai-org/os-ai-map/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) records each
method change against the release that made it. Coverage also grows release over release, so a
product missing from an early release was usually not yet scored rather than absent.

## What is not included

- **The long tail.** The map counts tens of thousands of discovered repositories, models and
  packages it has not scored. That inventory draws on third-party catalogs and is left out.
- **Preliminary categories.** Categories still being populated are not on the published map and
  are not here.
- **Raw adoption measurements.** Download counts and other observations live in the warehouse;
  this dataset carries the curated level and band.

## Limitations

Scores are curated judgments against a published rubric, made by people and reviewed agents, and
checked against cited sources. They are not measurements. Closed products are included
deliberately, so open options can be compared against them. Adoption is hard to observe for some
kinds of product (hosted services, hardware, apps), and the note on each score says what its
instrument can and cannot show.

Corrections are welcome as
[issues](https://github.com/currentai-org/os-ai-map/issues/new?template=report-an-error.yml) or
pull requests against `sources/` in the source repository.
